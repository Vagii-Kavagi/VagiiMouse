# Vagii Mouse privacy policy

Vagii Mouse does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, ads or tracking.
- Its settings, and any backups of your Windows mouse settings it makes, stay on your PC in
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\VagiiMouse`. Uninstalling removes the app; you can delete that folder at any time.
- The Microsoft Store version makes no network connections. The GitHub version contacts
  `api.github.com` only to check this project's releases for updates (this can be turned off
  in Settings). Nothing about you or your PC is sent.
- It reads mouse and touchpad movement only to apply your pointer speed, in memory, as it
  happens. Movement is never recorded or saved.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Vagii-Kavagi/VagiiMouse/issues
