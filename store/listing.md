# Store listing text (copy into Partner Center → Store listings → English)

## Description
Go past Windows' 20-step pointer speed. Vagii Mouse gives your mouse and your trackpad 100 speed
levels each, applied instantly, so you can tune a high-DPI feel without special hardware or
drivers.

Levels 1–20 use Windows' own pointer speed. Levels 21–100 add Vagii's boost on top, up to six
times Windows' maximum. Mouse and trackpad keep separate settings, and Vagii Mouse tells them
apart automatically.

- 100 speed levels for the mouse and for the trackpad, applied as you turn the dial
- Effective DPI readout based on your mouse's own DPI
- Acceleration: off for aiming, Windows' "enhance pointer precision", or Vagii's boost curve
  that speeds up fast flicks while keeping slow moves precise
- DPI / speed stages: save your favourite levels and switch with Ctrl+Alt+S from anywhere
- Ctrl+Alt+M turns the boost on or off instantly
- Pauses itself in games that hide or lock the cursor
- One-click reset to Windows defaults, restore your original settings, or reset the Windows
  mouse registry values (backed up first)
- Runs quietly in the tray; optional start with Windows
- No admin rights, no drivers, no data collection
